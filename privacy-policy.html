Privacy notice

What personal data Hrshop collects, why, and how it is stored. Written plainly, without lawyerisms.

Who this notice is from

This notice is from Hrshop editorial, Hrshop Editorial, 8 Bishop's Yard, Studio 3, Bristol BS1 4AA, United Kingdom. For privacy questions, contact privacy@hrshop.com.

What data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter: your email address only.
  • Server logs: IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — retained for 30 days for security and debugging.
  • Cookies: only technical cookies needed for the site to work. Optional analytics or marketing cookies are not set unless you accept them via the cookie banner.

Why we collect it

  • Contact form data — to reply to your message. Deleted within 12 months if no ongoing thread.
  • Newsletter email — to send you the monthly newsletter. Deleted immediately if you unsubscribe.
  • Server logs — for security and debugging, deleted after 30 days.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not run behavioural advertising.
  • We do not build profiles for targeted content.
  • We do not set analytics or marketing cookies without your consent.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Email privacy@hrshop.com and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority; in the UK, the ICO.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent (for newsletter and optional cookies) and on legitimate interests (for contact-form replies and short-term server logs).

A note about cookies

Hrshop stores only what is needed for the site to work. Optional cookies are not set until you accept. Read our cookie policy for details.
