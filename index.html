How we work

Editorial principles

01

Free to read

Every article on Hrshop is free to read. There are no paid subscriptions, no gated content, no courses, no coaching services, no affiliate links, and no plans to introduce them. Nothing is sold here. If that ever changes, we will say so on the site itself.

02

Patient

One or two pieces a month. Each one is edited slowly and given time to be right rather than fast. We would rather publish less well than more badly.

03

Not advice

Hrshop is an editorial magazine, not a legal or professional-HR service. Nothing on the site is legal, employment-law or professional HR advice. For questions about your specific situation, consult a qualified employment lawyer or HR professional in your jurisdiction.

A note about cookies

Hrshop stores only what is needed for the site to work. Optional cookies are not set until you accept. Read our cookie policy for details.
