An independent English-language editorial magazine about everyday workplace practices — hiring conversations, onboarding, feedback rituals, one-to-ones, meeting etiquette, remote-work daily rhythms and team communication. Hrshop is a magazine about working together. Nothing is sold here. Not a recruiting service, not an HR consultancy, not a software product.
