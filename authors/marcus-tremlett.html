Marcus Tremlett

Marcus Tremlett writes the meetings and remote-work desk at Hrshop. He came to writing about workplaces the long way, through several years of running small distributed teams and one very patient chair of the board, and now writes about ordinary team practice with the calm of someone who has finally stopped believing that the next meeting-tool will fix his calendar. His pieces are careful with attention, honest about the cost of meetings, and quietly opposed to the idea that remote work has to be complicated to work. Marcus is a fictional composite editorial persona for Hrshop; he does not represent a real individual and does not offer real HR or legal advice.
