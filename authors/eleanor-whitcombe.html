Eleanor Whitcombe

Eleanor Whitcombe edits Hrshop and writes most of the hiring, onboarding and one-to-one pieces. She learned the craft of working with people slowly, over years of being on both sides of the desk in small teams, and now spends most of her working life thinking about the shape of an ordinary Tuesday at work — the one-to-one, the stand-up, the quiet difficult conversation. Her writing is patient, undramatic and, she hopes, useful. She is a fictional composite editorial persona created for Hrshop; she does not represent a real individual and does not offer real HR or legal advice. She writes from Bristol.
