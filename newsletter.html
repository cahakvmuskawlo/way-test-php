The Hrshop newsletter

One quiet email a month, with new articles and small reading notes. No offers, no urgency, no affiliate links, no course upsells.

What you get

  • A short round-up of the month's new pieces, with plain descriptions.
  • Occasional reading notes from the editors on workplace practice.
  • Editor's notes on what we're working on or asking readers about.

What you don't get

  • Sales messages, promotions or coupon codes.
  • Sponsored content presented as editorial.
  • Course upsells, coaching offers, or recruiting-service pitches.
  • Emails sold or shared with third parties.

You can unsubscribe from every email with the link in the footer. If we ever change how the newsletter works, we'll tell subscribers before it changes.

A note about cookies

Hrshop stores only what is needed for the site to work. Optional cookies are not set until you accept. Read our cookie policy for details.

Hrshop

An independent English-language editorial magazine about everyday workplace practices — hiring conversations, onboarding, feedback rituals, one-to-ones, meeting etiquette, remote-work daily rhythms and team communication. Hrshop is a magazine about working together. Nothing is sold here. Not a recruiting service, not an HR consultancy, not a software product. Hrshop is chosen as the name of this editorial publication and is not affiliated with any real-world HR company, consultancy, software product or business that may share a similar name.
