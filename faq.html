Frequently asked questions

Things readers often ask about Hrshop, how we work, and how we don't.

What is Hrshop?

Hrshop is an independent English-language editorial magazine about everyday workplace practices — hiring conversations, onboarding, feedback rituals, one-to-ones, meetings, remote-work rhythms and the small honest habits that quietly hold a working team together. Every article is free to read. Hrshop is a free reading site — there are no paid subscriptions, no courses, no coaching, no recruiting products, no HR software, no affiliate links, and no products for sale.

Is Hrshop a shop?

No. Despite the name, Hrshop is a magazine about working together, not a shop. There is no cart, no checkout, no marketplace, no products for sale, no paid downloads, no courses, no coaching, no subscriptions. Nothing is sold here. The name is our own editorial choice.

Is Hrshop a recruiting service, HR consultancy, or software product?

No. Hrshop is a magazine. It is not a recruiting agency, not an HR consultancy, not an HR software product, not a marketplace, not a SaaS platform, and not a service business. We do not place candidates, do not consult on HR programmes, do not sell software, and do not offer any paid service. We publish articles you can read for free.

Are you affiliated with any organisation or business of a similar name?

No. Hrshop, in this context, is an editorial project chosen as the name of this publication. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any real-world HR company, HR consultancy, HR software product, recruiting service, marketplace or business that may share a similar name. We do not use their logos, materials, catalogue, or branding. Anything they do is not our doing, and anything we publish is not theirs.

Does Hrshop offer HR advice, employment-law advice, or professional guidance for my situation?

No. Hrshop is a magazine about workplace practices, not a legal or professional-HR service. Nothing on Hrshop is legal advice, employment-law advice, professional HR advice, or guidance about your specific situation. For questions about your specific situation — employment law, workplace disputes, hiring decisions, redundancy, discipline, or anything else that would benefit from qualified professional input — consult a qualified employment lawyer or HR professional in your jurisdiction whose situation and legal context match yours.

Do you offer courses, coaching, certifications, or paid downloads?

No. Hrshop is a free reading site. There are no paid courses, no gated content, no coaching services, no certifications, no accreditation, no memberships, no paid downloads, no e-books, and no products for sale. We are a magazine you can read, and that is all.

Do you accept sponsored content?

No. There is no sponsored content, no advertising and no paid product placement anywhere on Hrshop. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first and any sponsored piece will be clearly labelled.

Do you use affiliate links or recommend specific products?

No. There are no affiliate links, no prices, no shopping links, no product recommendations, and no software endorsements anywhere on the site. Where we discuss tools or approaches, we discuss the practice itself, not what to buy.

Do you publish salary numbers or pay benchmarks?

No. Hrshop does not publish salary numbers, pay benchmarks, compensation data or specific pay figures of any kind. Salary questions are jurisdiction- and situation-specific and belong with a qualified professional who knows your context.

Do you name real companies as case studies?

No. Every example on Hrshop is anonymised or a fictional composite. We do not use the names of real companies, real employees or real teams as case studies. Any names that appear in an article are illustrative and fictional.

How do I report an error?

Email corrections@hrshop.com or use the contact form. Genuine corrections are made in place, and significant updates are noted at the foot of the piece.

How can I pitch an article?

Send a short outline to pitch@hrshop.com — a paragraph on the idea, why it fits Hrshop, and a note about your background. We reply within a few weeks.

How often is Hrshop published?

One or two pieces a month, plus a monthly newsletter. We would rather publish less well than more badly.

What happens when I subscribe to the newsletter?

You receive one email a month with new articles and quiet reading notes. No shopping links, no offers, no urgency, no promotions. You can unsubscribe at any time; instructions are in every email.

Where is Hrshop based?

The editorial office is in Bristol, United Kingdom. Visits are by prior arrangement only.

I am dealing with a difficult workplace situation. Can Hrshop help?

Hrshop publishes patient, general editorial writing about workplace practices. It is not a substitute for professional advice. If you are dealing with a difficult workplace situation — bullying, discrimination, redundancy, mental-health impact, or anything requiring qualified input — consult a qualified employment lawyer, HR professional, or where appropriate a doctor or mental-health professional. In the UK, ACAS provides free confidential guidance on workplace matters; other jurisdictions have equivalent services.

How do you handle personal data?

Only what we need — a name and email if you contact us or subscribe to the newsletter. See our privacy notice for the full picture.

A note about cookies

Hrshop stores only what is needed for the site to work. Optional cookies are not set until you accept. Read our cookie policy for details.
